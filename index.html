About the audio

The voices in this app are generated by AI, not recorded by a person. They are made with VieNeu-TTS, an open Vietnamese speech model, and checked against PhoWhisper. No real person's voice is imitated. The syllable list comes from Luong Hieu Thi's corpus study.

Synthetic speech is good enough to train your ear on these contrasts, but it is not a native speaker. If something sounds wrong to you, check with one.
